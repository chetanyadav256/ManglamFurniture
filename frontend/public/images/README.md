# Sample photography

Local WebP images downloaded from Unsplash's image CDN for the illustrative catalog. They are not photographs of Manglam Furniture's actual products or showroom. The exact source photo identifiers are recorded in `sources.json`; a source URL has the form `https://images.unsplash.com/photo-{identifier}`.

Replace these images with verified product and showroom photography before presenting this catalog as real inventory. Product galleries currently show a full photo and explicitly labelled detail/texture crops of that photo.

Local files avoid third-party image requests during browsing. Large interior images are 1800 pixels wide; other photos are 1000 pixels wide. Cards use lazy loading, and the main hero/product photos load eagerly.
